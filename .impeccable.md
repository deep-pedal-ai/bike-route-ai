## Design Context

### Users
Bike Route AI is for all cyclists: casual riders, commuters, fitness riders, route nerds, and people exploring unfamiliar roads or trails. They use it to generate route ideas and inspect a live route corpus on a map before deciding what feels rideable.

### Brand Personality
Playful, outdoorsy, and helpful. The product should feel like a friendly AI riding companion with useful data, not a severe GIS console or a neon cyber dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Fresh outdoor palette with leaf, meadow, sky, clay, and trail tones. Lime can remain as a living green accent, but it should feel botanical rather than electric. Avoid neon glow effects and bike-unrelated sci-fi styling.

### Design Principles
- Make the map feel like terrain and route discovery, not a dark trading terminal.
- Use warm outdoor contrast: leaf greens, sky blues, clay/wildflower accents, and softened charcoal surfaces.
- Keep data legible and instrument-like without making monospace the whole personality.
- Prefer purposeful line casing, borders, and color contrast over glow effects.
- Keep controls friendly enough for all cyclists while preserving enough density for serious route inspection.
